How It Works

From application to Demo Day:

  • Step#01: Apply Online Submit your pitch deck and complete a short application form to get started.
  • Step#02: Screening & Technical Review Our team evaluates your idea, technology, and market fit to ensure alignment.
  • Step#03: Founder Interviews Selected founders are invited for interviews to assess vision, execution, and team strength.
  • Step#04: Program Kickoff & Weekly Sprints The program begins with structured sprints, mentorship, and hands-on execution.
  • Step#05: Demo Day & Follow-On Introductions Present your startup to investors and partners with curated post-program introductions.
